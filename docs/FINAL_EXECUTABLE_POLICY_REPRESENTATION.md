# Final Executable Policy Representation

## Executive verdict

**Select a resource-contracted, parameterized service-tour programme, compiled offline into timed worker/market schedules and a bounded BKG4 branching controller.** This is candidate F below: a high-fidelity schedule compiler, not an online target-to-task scheduler, a bag of interchangeable days, or a residual action overlay.

**THIS IS THE REPRESENTATION WE SHOULD BUILD NEXT.**

Its central object is a complete economic programme: explicit acquisitions, asset lifecycles, worker tours, daily hiring/spawn programmes, cargo transfers, ordered financing/sale programmes, and observation-guarded alternative continuations. A tour keeps a worker on a sequence of related sites and performs the whole service visit before moving on. An offline compiler binds these programmes to workers, sites and time, checking their joint resource contracts. It must preserve strong service sequences, not rediscover movement and service ordering from generic economic targets.

The selection is **a falsifiable engineering judgment, not a demonstrated solution**. No inspected representation has established all five requested properties together: >=95% Shop Plus productivity, eight coordinated economies, adaptation, exact CPU/CUDA execution, and faithful BKG4 deployment. The most important unresolved risk is whether the compiler can preserve elite coordination without becoming a disguised complete tape. The first experiment must test that question before any learned discovery.

The generic plan-target/task-executive interface was **falsified at `180074ee2e3c96f6798771c0032c9297383e3a5f`**. [FINAL_POLICY_DISCOVERY_ARCHITECTURE.md](FINAL_POLICY_DISCOVERY_ARCHITECTURE.md) remains useful high-level research, but its selected executable interface is invalidated. Neither its optimizer nor its optimizer fallback is authorized by this representation decision. That document is intentionally unchanged.

**One fallback:** candidate B, an event-driven BKG4 skill/FSM library of complete service tours, with explicit ownership of ongoing obligations. Trigger it only if source-derived tours retain competence but calendar binding fails specifically under legitimate timing variation. It is not permission to retry a generic scheduler, a frozen tape portfolio, or neural execution.

**Immediate experiment:** one <=1,000-native-FGE representation preflight, with the same language, compiler and runtime for a reconstructed elite-equivalent programme and eight materially different programmes. A reference executable is a comparator only. No implementation, simulation campaign, learning, champion change, submission or push is part of this report.

The [executive source](../native/bkg4/include/economic_plan_preflight.hpp) specifies targets, rank-based site allocation, fixed task priorities, one retained task per worker, distance-weighted selection, and a fixed market purchasing order. In particular, `choose_task()` prioritizes individual service tasks, `realize_task()` retrieves feed independently and moves x-first, and `market()` orders land, hires, seeds, animals and feed through a fixed routine. A high-level target does not specify an economically coordinated tour, spawn sequence, financing race or lifecycle schedule.

Several execution burdens are visible in this source: service types compete independently; collection has a fixed low priority; carried goods trigger return-to-shed work from hour 20 even though the engine deposits all cargo at dawn; site categories and input-buy order are structurally imposed. These are **mechanistic explanations worth testing**, not isolated causal ablations proving which one caused the cash collapse. Adding a priority knob would not establish that the missing coordination has been recovered.

The hypothesis rejected is: **this generic deterministic executive, driven by economic targets, could reproduce near-elite execution at the bounded preflight budget**. It does not reject every possible scheduler, all hierarchy, symbolic policies, BKG4 capacity, exact simulation or future learning.

## Why elite execution is difficult

The [C++ reference simulator](../native/cpp/include/sim.hpp), especially `step_with_unit_counts`, `apply_unit_actions`, `do_hire`, `end_of_day`, and `daily_refresh_and_spawn`, establishes the relevant ordering:

1. Existing workers act, in worker-index order. Aggregate PLANT requests can block every planting of that crop if requested demand exceeds seed inventory, including requests beyond the currently acting workforce.
2. Market orders execute. New hires, seeds, land and animals therefore cannot support an earlier unit action in the same turn.
3. Town consumption and prices update; crop decay is applied.
4. At dawn, biology updates, cargo is deposited in worker/inventory-insertion order, hands disappear, the farmer returns to `(4,4)`, and daily hire count resets. Shops may unlock.

Daily hiring is not permanent workforce growth: the labour curve is a sequence of **daily re-hiring programmes**. Hire spawns use the least-occupied of four shed-access positions, with deterministic ties. Altering a farmer move or the timing/order of hires changes subsequent worker starting positions. A strategy cannot swap worker IDs without preserving this relationship.

Crop and livestock obligations survive dawn. Cows first yield after eight days and then every two, sheep after six and then every three, geese after four and then daily. Feed/care history changes subsequent yield; two unfed days can lose an animal. Watering, finite-crop age, perennial yield and decay clocks also matter. These are verified native definitions, not an economic ranking of species. [Engine definitions](../native/cpp/include/sim.hpp)

The 100-unit shed is a shared resource. A market sale occurs too late to make room for a same-turn worker deposit. End-of-day auto-deposit means returning every worker is unnecessary, but capacity must already be available. The final actionable turn is 718, before the usual last-day dawn, so terminal cargo needs explicit realization. Fixed unit service, transport and market slot ordering are coupled.

Consequently, a good representation must carry **temporal resource commitments**, not just intended outcomes. A pasture purchase creates a placement job, feed procurement, recurring service time, cargo load, market realization and future labour financing. A mechanically legal purchase can still destroy the productive schedule.

### What makes it productive

Shop interleaves service operations at the same location and uses deliberately timed acquisition and seed-buy sequences. The route audit finds many repetitions of `FEED, CARE, COLLECT_FERTILIZER` embedded in movement tours, and `WATER, HARVEST, PLANT WHEAT, WATER` renewal visits. This reduces repeated approach costs and carries inputs for several actions, rather than reconsidering each task globally.

For route 0, the first turn orders a WHEAT buy/sell/buy sequence; the next turn orders a WHEAT sale and repurchase, five hires, two cows and two sheep. Subsequent seed purchases are spread across the day. This **ordered financing programme**, not merely four animal targets, determines what workers can do next. The analysis does not assume the buy/sell sequence is free profit; its actual value depends on engine ordering and the live shared market.

The proven runtime preserves inventory insertion order, projects this turn's reachable shed transfers, leads selected next-turn sales, subtracts led quantities without removing market slots, and applies exact terminal ordering. `advance_sales()` excludes WHEAT/FERTILIZER and will not lead across a 72-step boundary or on a current step divisible by four. These are declared sale-strategy rules over a reusable mechanical projection, not a generic profit optimizer.

The [mechanics fallback](../workspace/self_improvement/mechanics_fallback/README.md) found no overflowing reference DROP opportunities on its diagnostic panel and only seven discarded low-price units per game from overnight deposit. Feed-preservation overlays interfered with deliberate sell-and-buy-back financing. That evidence argues against replacing Shop's entire cargo/market programme with apparently safer generic rules.

### Separation limits and safe parameterization

**Reusable unchanged in semantics:** item/action mapping; ordered quantities and omitted-argument handling; legal observation copying; inventory-order-aware shed projection; the bounded weed-insertion protocol; advanced-sale accounting; deterministic serialization and terminal projection. For a new programme, the policy must explicitly select sale/repair modes. These helpers must not select herd composition or invent investments.

**Strategy-bearing:** crop/herd acquisition time, sites, service cadence, fertilizer use, per-day hiring time and count, stock reservation, planned sales and financing order, and land purchase timing. Worker tour order is partly strategy: it allocates scarce time between economic commitments.

**Changes that can sometimes avoid rescheduling:** disabling an unused sale lead, changing a sale quantity with a proven inventory/financing reserve, or selecting an already validated continuation with matching own-asset obligations. Even these are conditional permissions, not universally safe mutations.

**Changes requiring schedule reconstruction:** a different opening, earlier/later hire, changed land date, altered animal maturity phase, new crop replacement cycle, substantial herd mix change, a service worker reassignment, or different geometry. Rebuild affected tours, input transfers and funding successors together. Do not reuse the old worker's service lane just because COW and SHEEP both occupy a pasture.

**Structural assumptions:** four central shed-access cells; hire-spawn occupancy ordering; worker-index and inventory-key ordering; the day reset; field access; exact service locations; 13 output worker slots in Shop's tape format; <=10 market orders. The simulation and BKG4 allow 40 units, but the packed route supports only 12 hands. These are different limits.

**How much survives a new herd/crop mix?** The small runtime mechanisms can survive largely unchanged. The daily feed/care/collection visit shape can serve multiple species, but harvest phase, procurement, financing, placement and tour membership must change. Crop renewal versus perennial programmes need different lifecycles. There is no defensible measured percentage of the *whole actuator* that survives; short motif repetition is not that percentage. The preflight must measure retained productive behaviour.

Four-action motif windows overlap; their counts must not be summed into a compression ratio. Slot equality includes PASS and compares literal worker index, not an optimal role matching. Day packets carry market actions and exact list arity. These definitions deliberately avoid overstating semantic reuse.

The first-day five hires occur at hour 1. In later days, hires are concentrated in hours 0 and 1, but the split varies. For example day 10 requests ten at hour 0 and two at hour 1; day 11 requests five and five. A single daily workforce target misses this occupancy-sensitive spawn schedule. Sales are distributed across the day: route 0 has 38 SELL requests at hour 21 and 22 at hour 23, versus 46 at hour 1, aggregated over the season. It is not simply an end-of-day seller.

**Important correction to a tempting simplification:** the stored 13 tapes have a common prefix of only **70** actions because old route 1 differs at step 70. The deployed controller nevertheless uses route 0 until 144. Routes 3-12 first differ from route 0 at 144; many first differ in worker actions at 152. Therefore neither copying route 1 from reset nor counting 13 tapes demonstrates 13 legal opening choices. Route 2 also differs in stored earlier rows before it is selected at 648. Stored tapes and the actually routed policy are different objects.

All tapes request zero TOMATO planting. Eleven have 163 WHEAT, 31 CARROT, 33 STRAWBERRY and 12 MELON plant requests; routes 9/12 have 165 WHEAT and 28 CARROT. Herd requests differ more: route 12 requests four cows and fourteen sheep, whereas route 0 requests eight cows, six sheep and three geese. This is evidence that service programmes can support different animal mixes, not proof that arbitrary substitutions retain value. Separate read-only Gold counts show the same crop request profile, land steps 150/265, and 260 hires in routes 0/2/3 versus 277 in route 1.

The supplied representative produced vector (517 wheat, 84 carrot, 249 strawberry, 72 melon, 78 egg, 245 milk, 161 wool, 364 fertilizer) is **not independently reproduced by the committed productivity summary or this static audit**. It is not treated here as a measured universal Shop profile. The 260 hire requests are independently verified. Future preflight telemetry must record actual yields, not infer them from route opcodes.

### Archive interpretation

The existing [archive audit](../workspace/research/top10_replay_archive/evidence.json) is pinned to version 19, spanning July 30-September 8, with 348 sampled episodes and 527 proxy-selected player trajectories. Its 521 distinct full-action hashes contrast with only 2.927 effective coarse herd regimes. It contains 224 four-checkpoint asset profiles, 66 tomato-request trajectories and 68 trajectories with geese at observation 576. These are structural signals, not 224 independent good policies.

Its detailed 41-game sample records up to 15 hands; 31 of 82 player trajectories exceeded 12. Hence a universal new executor restricted to the old 12-hand pack would have a real representational limitation. Checkpoint-0-hour workforce counts are misleading because hands have just been dismissed. The archive's few matched-observation disagreements over the *next 72 turns* cannot identify the generating policy or prove causal online switching.

No raw replay was copied or newly decoded in this session. Prefer source semantics for execution. Use archive evidence to challenge missing lifecycle/geometry support, never replay identity as a runtime state or public replays as unauthorized BUILD labels.

Recent-work searches covered 2024-2026 executable skills, hierarchical programme policies, symbolic extraction and composition. The inspected 2025/2026 papers strengthen the case for **jointly specified executable bodies and maintenance contracts**, not for introducing a neural low-level executor now. Their domain results do not supply a quantitative prior for Shop retention.

### Learned actuator and induction assessment

Conditional imitation would need demonstrations with explicit commands, not just primitive action histories. The same visible farm can call for different actions under different long-lived commitments. Replays generally omit those commitments; inferring them is part of the problem. Per-worker independent cloning also fails to enforce joint seeds, shed capacity, financing order or action-slot ownership.

Even an illustrative 99% per-turn joint-action match gives about 7.19 expected deviations across 719 turns, and under an unrealistic independent-error model only `0.99^719 ~= 0.00073` probability of no deviation. This is not a return bound: some deviations are harmless, others ruin an acquisition. It explains why held-out classification accuracy is the wrong actuator gate.

DAgger needs a teacher that can execute the commanded alternate economy after mistakes. Querying the original Shop tape at an incompatible new farm does not provide that teacher. Recurrent policies introduce unobserved hidden memory that then needs a finite, faithful BKG4 realization. A quantized neural runtime could be deterministic but is explicitly not an allowed substitute for executable BKG4 policy. Learning the actuator now would move the least-understood problem into the component hardest to deploy.

Pure programme induction is also underdetermined. A decision tree can explain one trace by testing STEP and returning its next action. A source-aware extractor should instead identify service visits, parameter bindings and obligations, then test them under fresh interventions. For this task, manual/source-guided factoring plus a small deterministic compiler is a cheaper first falsification than implementing general FSM induction, symbolic regression or a neural synthesizer. Later induction may propose new templates in the same language.

## Candidate representations

All estimates below are judgments for a focused first implementation, not measurements or probabilities. All candidates must face the same reproduction/diversity gate and the same legal-observation firewall. Strategic expressiveness means **realized** economies, not the number of legal parameter strings.

### A. Contract-safe elite schedule blocks

Policy: a guarded graph of fixed high-fidelity day, three-day or larger phase blocks. Contracts record own layout/assets and ages, cash/input assumptions, worker/spawn requirements, pending repairs, sales debt and maintenance handoff. BKG4 chooses blocks; immutable records supply execution; BB holds block/phase and node state handles bounds; helper state holds queues and sale ledgers.

It best preserves existing schedules when used in place, but composition may have very low coverage. One-day blocks reset workers, not asset ages. Three-day blocks reduce some phase ambiguity but still span ongoing crops and cows. An opening/financing block transfers an entire future obligation set. A complete lifecycle may span most of the game. Harvest/deposit or worker-service blocks are more reusable but need shared-resource scheduling, at which point this approaches F.

New openings require genuinely new blocks plus compatible downstream economies; relabeling all old days is still the same tape. Observation-conditioned selection is cheap, CUDA exactness straightforward and snapshots moderate. Data needs are small if contracts come from source, much greater if inferred from traces. Pilot estimate 3-6 days; branching is sparse after contract filtering. High fixed-route-support and Shop-overfit risk, low generic-scheduler risk. **Rank 3**: strong fidelity, weaker evidence that compatible macro recombination spans eight economies.

### B. BKG4-native executable skill/FSM library

Policy: an event-driven programme of full acquisition and multi-site service skills, not primitive-task priorities. Each skill owns a worker/tour, input/capacity reservation and maintenance obligation until a checked handoff. Explicit FSM state dispatches shared BKG4 subtrees; no VM subroutine-call opcode is assumed. Parameters bind sites, species, worker roles and bounded service modes.

This permits new openings and conditional recovery without relying on a particular timestamp. Finance, land, labour and production are coordinated by a predeclared dependency graph and reserved service lanes, not a greedy priority executive. The disadvantage is altered timing: a locally successful reactive skill can delay every later purchase and market race. It must retain waits and complete service sequences where the source requires them.

Exact integer CPU/CUDA execution and direct deployment are plausible, with more BB/node state and visits than F. Source-derived skills require no neural data, but need off-nominal tests. Pilot estimate 7-10 days from scratch; a narrowly triggered fallback can reuse F's contracts/tours. Branching is `skill x parameters x eligible resources`, constrained by ownership. Moderate generic-scheduler risk if ownership slips, medium Shop-overfit risk, low fixed-tape risk. **Rank 2 and sole fallback.**

### C. Residual strategic control over the current Shop actuator

Policy: preserve Shop's current `tick` and tape system, adding strategic decisions over purchases, staffing, composition, land and sales. In zero-residual mode this reproduces Shop; BB and nodes encode overrides, native state retains Shop queues and sales debt.

The source does not expose the requested strategic control surface. Editing a hire changes spawn positions, and editing a herd changes all its future service. An unchanged worker tape cannot realize general crop/herd/layout commands. Small market residuals are plausible but fail the mission's breadth; large residuals require rebuilding the schedule, turning this into F rather than remaining C.

CUDA/snapshot implementation would initially be easy; high-fidelity reproduction could be immediate, but only by retaining the full original tape. No amount of residual-tree accuracy establishes useful new openings. Pilot estimate 1-3 days to falsify breadth; explosive invalid branching when economic edits affect lanes. Very high local-edit and Shop-overfit risks. **Rank 7; reject for this task**, despite being attractive for ordinary narrow repairs.

### D. Multi-family executable programme grammar

Policy: family-specific nonterminals for opening finance, expansion, herd/crop service and realization, composing only at declared semantic interfaces. Each family retains its own actuator conventions. BKG4 lowers the grammar to explicit dispatch; programme tables retain bodies; family memory is namespaced and snapshotted.

Potentially broader than a single compiler and more faithful than universal online arbitration. But available strong sources are mostly related tapes with different wrappers, not independently authored reusable generators. Shared grammar types cannot make incompatible herd ages/layouts interchangeable. Introducing many family-specific runtimes greatly increases engineering and parity costs.

Pilot estimate 8-12 days; source/license and semantic inventory needs high; branching multiplicative across grammar choices but few crossings may be valid. Moderate fixed-route risk and lower Shop-overfit risk if truly different source families exist. **Rank 4**. Use family-specific template contributions inside F only after their same-language reproduction passes; do not build a general multi-runtime grammar first.

### E. Learned low-level actuator plus strategic policy

Policy: a command-conditioned multiworker model generates actions and memory transitions. It would need to be extracted into BKG4 conditions/FSMs and bounded programme bodies, with all recurrent state explicitly reconstructed. No neural runtime may remain outside the VM.

The command/action interface could in principle express every requested economic dimension and adaptation. In practice the training data lacks alternate-command labels, joint resource failures are costly, and off-policy supervision is unavailable from a fixed elite tape. CUDA neural inference can be deterministic in a fixed stack, but that is not exact parity with a later symbolic student.

Pilot estimate 2-4 weeks or more, substantial conditional data and expert-query requirements, very large action branching. High distillation, distribution-shift and implicit-generic-scheduler risk. It may avoid Shop bias with sufficiently varied competent teachers, which are not currently available. **Rank 8; reject now.**

### F. Parameterized service-tour schedule compiler

Policy: asset programmes with lifecycle templates, worker tours, explicit financial precedences and guarded schedule alternatives. Each tour supplies an ordered high-fidelity service sequence and geometry. The compiler allocates intervals and market slots jointly, using source-derived schedule witnesses as preferences, not immutable inherited lanes. A compiled action calendar plus BKG4 guards is the executable policy. This addresses research tracks F/G with **source-guided programme reconstruction**, not a claim to solve general induction.

It preserves short high-throughput sequences and economic timing while exposing opening, acquisition, workforce, land, service, layout and realization parameters. Unlike A it can build new schedules rather than selecting only donor blocks. Unlike B, expensive binding is offline and the runtime does not improvise new worker assignments each turn. Unlike C, it can replace the opening and every downstream resource allocation.

Exact CUDA execution is plausible because the candidate evaluated is already the lowered programme. Snapshots contain explicit cursors/queues and BKG4 state, not an optimizer. No neural data is needed for the initial reconstruction. Pilot estimate 6-8 focused days; nominal combinatorics can exceed millions, but feasible, productive diversity is unknown. Main risks are disguising the full tape as parameters and building a generic scheduler inside the compiler. **Rank 1; selected subject to strict anti-disguise and productivity gates.**

### G. Direct causal programme/FSM induction

Policy: infer a compact reactive programme from source and annotated execution, using decision-tree extraction, symbolic synthesis or trace-to-FSM inference. This differs from F because the learner must discover the execution language/body rather than binding an explicit engineering sketch. An accepted result directly lowers to BKG4; source templates, BB and finite memory account for all state.

Potentially excellent strategic generality, but passive traces do not determine causal rules or contracts. Source Shop itself does not contain its generating scheduler. VIPER-style extraction can preserve an expert without making it command-conditioned. Meaningful induction needs interventions and a substantial new methodology before the first productivity gate.

Pilot estimate 2-3 weeks, high data/specification requirements; program branching combinatorial; exact final CUDA semantics possible but extraction/reconstruction uncertain. Very high trace-memorization risk, medium generic-scheduler and Shop-overfit risks. **Rank 6**. Source-guided factoring is useful now; a general induction system is not selected.

### H. Multi-family complete executable programme population

Policy: several complete coherent families, selected from reset or at certified shared boundaries; each keeps its original actuator. BKG4 hosts a small selector and data; each family's complete persistent state must be accounted for. This maximizes in-family fidelity and avoids arbitrary cross-family mixing.

Its main weakness is discovery: loading eight strong policies is not expressing eight novel economies. Available public families are related, and a new complete economy still requires a generator or a competent full-policy author. Blindly switching from one complete programme into another is unsafe; from-reset selection at identical initial legal observations cannot produce reactive diversity unless distinct candidate policies are evaluated offline.

Pilot estimate 4-8 days if eight executable families already exist, which is not established; new families take longer. Finite family branching is cheap, but data and exact CPU/CUDA ports multiply. Full-tape retention would violate this task's anti-disguise criterion. **Rank 5**, useful for deployment portfolios after discovery, not the next discovery representation.

## Final selected representation

**Resource-contracted parameterized service-tour programmes, with offline schedule compilation and BKG4-controlled observation branches.** The compilation unit is an economic programme, not a mutated tape. The scheduling unit is a **service tour or acquisition/finance programme** that already contains its detailed actuator behaviour, not one generic task.

There is one language and one runtime for the reference-like reconstruction and every novel economy. Reusing Shop's mechanical helper semantics is encouraged. Calling the original Shop policy to supply otherwise missing actions is forbidden in the new representation. The frozen reference remains available only on the comparator side of evaluation.

The compiler's objective in the first preflight is feasibility and fidelity, not selecting the best RL/search method. It may deterministically choose among bindings according to **candidate-specified** order/slack preferences. It must not hide economic heuristics that decide which crop, herd or investment is profitable. If a strategy cannot be scheduled, reject it with the resource conflict; do not quietly replace its economy.

## Exact policy object and contracts

### Policy object

The following is a design specification, not an implemented schema:

```text
ExecutableEconomy
  engine/feature ABI IDs, source provenance, semantic hash
  asset programmes: site, item, acquisition, lifespan, recurring service
  financing programmes: ordered orders, reserves, receipt dependencies
  labour programmes: daily hire times/order, spawn assumptions, tour roles
  tours: ordered sites, concrete service-visit template, path template
  realization programmes: deposits, dawn capacity, sales and terminal exit
  boundary graph: legal predicates -> complete compatible continuations
  failure graph: bounded repair, obligation-preserving reduced programme
  compiled calendar: bindings/intervals/order slots, generated from the above
  BKG4 controller: economic branch decisions and explicit mode/latch state
```

Start with at most 64 asset-programme descriptors and 16 simultaneously addressed workers, including the farmer. The language must admit all five crops and three animal species, with fertilizer and cargo programmes; a crop or species is admissible only after its concrete acquisition/service/lifecycle template exists. Support 40 units in the underlying ABI without pretending the 16-worker pilot covers that range. Reject larger pilot programmes rather than silently truncating.

A service tour contains a bounded ordered list of sites, a path choice and full on-site visit variants, for example input pickup -> travel -> feed -> care -> fertilizer collection -> conditional harvest -> next site. It may deliberately leave cargo for automatic dawn deposit. A crop programme binds preparation, seed availability, planting, water/fertilizer visits, maturity-aware harvest and finite/perennial continuation. A finance programme binds complete order sequences, not an unordered set of desired purchases.

### Entry, maintenance and exit contracts

| Contract field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Entry clock | Day/hour or bounded interval, and biological phase constraints; not evaluation seed |
| Own geometry | Referenced sites, accessible quadrants, shed access, allowed weed uncertainty, required structures |
| Cash / inputs | Currently observable minimum cash, seeds, shed/cargo items, protected obligations, and conditional receipt dependencies |
| Workforce | Expected actual count, actor identity within day, spawn-position witness, retained cargo and tour ownership |
| Persistent assets | Species/crop, actual placement/planting age, yield/decay phase, deprivation/care/fertilizer state needed by this body |
| Pending state | Repair queue status, led-sale debt/due step, active programme IDs and cancellations |
| Market assumptions | Explicit visible price/inventory bands only where needed; no future shops or fixed opponent suffix |
| Maintenance | Named service deadlines, resource reservations, supported timing/weed variation and allowed repair slack |
| Exit | Observed completed effects, released intervals/resources, unresolved commitments transferred explicitly |
| Continuing obligations | Every live plant/herd service owner, future feed/seed procurement and realization; no silent deletion on branch change |

A module's precondition is not an assertion that all orders will fill or that it will earn a fixed cash amount. An exit is accepted only from observed effects. Purchases financed by preceding sales need an explicit success/failure continuation. Simultaneous market interleaving can change cash; use conservative guards and observe receipts before committing downstream actions that require them.

Contracts are **own-resource projections**, not complete simulator-state signatures. A service visit usually refers to one to four sites and one worker; a capital-phase contract references its dependency set. The composite may legitimately inspect many own tiles, but must not use a whole-world hash, RNG state, opponent private inventory or a future observation. Set an initial cap of 64 monitored assets and <=256 primitive contract checks at a strategic boundary. More checks require a new complexity decision, not an ever-growing hidden-state signature.

### Scheduling and ownership

The offline compiler builds a precedence/resource graph. Nodes are complete visit, acquisition, transfer, hire or ordered-market programmes. Edges include buy-before-pickup, unlock-before-use, seed-before-PLANT, feed-before-deprivation, service-before-yield opportunity, deposit-before-sale, and sale/receipt-before-funded-purchase. Worker intervals cannot overlap. Seed, input and shed reservations are shared, not per-skill estimates that can double-spend.

A schedule witness fixes worker/tour order, paths, waits and market slots. Source timings are initial preferred witnesses. Every binding can be replaced when compiling a new coherent programme; no immutable post-144 prefix or inherited-lane eligibility filter exists. A small fixed-order constraint checker is sufficient initially; use a proven integer solver such as CP-SAT only if variable binding actually requires it. Do not implement a new general optimizer or a duplicate economic simulator.

The compiler guarantees structural constraints under its declared entry/environment assumptions. It does **not** prove profitability or all future market affordability. The exact native engine supplies those outcomes, and observation guards handle assumption failure. Unknown prices cannot be replaced with the true future price from a simulation seed.

### Worked contract example

An acquisition programme for two sheep binds two empty pasture sites, two units of animal stock, the workers that pick them up, a path/tour, and subsequent daily service ownership. The buy is a market programme executed before the pickup turn, with cash >=1,000 plus protected reserves when funded directly. The first service and later harvest calendar depend on **observed placement day**, not the planned order timestamp. A late/missing fill selects a precompiled delayed-acquisition or maintenance-only continuation before those resources are spent elsewhere.

Changing the two animals to cows preserves pasture/placement mechanics but changes purchase cost, maturity lag and harvest periodicity. The compiler must regenerate finance, timed harvest visits, cargo/sale events and any conflicting service roles. It may keep a feed/care/collection visit unchanged, but cannot just relabel products on the old wool lane. This is the concrete distinction from the failed single-lifecycle mutation support.

### Persistent state

Keep programme/branch ID, phase, committed continuation, per-worker tour cursor and repair state, debt/due fields for advanced sales, and explicit failed/continuing obligation flags. Day boundaries reset only day-scoped actor/queue state. Long-lived asset state should normally be read from legal observations rather than copied into a competing hidden model. Memory of observed successful acquisition and the selected service programme is legitimate.

No persistent solver, neural activation, opponent model identity, Python closure or file lookup exists at runtime. Runtime state must be fully specified before the first snapshot test.

## How Shop-level productivity is preserved

The argument is source-level and conditional:

1. **Retain complete productive visits.** Do not split feed/care/fertilizer/harvest into competing global tasks. Use the repeatedly observed visit order and input-carry programme.
2. **Retain concrete movement and timing witnesses.** The reference-like programme starts from source-derived tours with the same direction order, intentional waits, hire slots and servicing phases. No x-first shortest-path replacement is imposed on its existing tours.
3. **Retain ordered financing.** Preserve the early market programme, later procurement dependencies, seed availability before multiworker planting, and market order slots. New economies change these as whole programmes, not quantity nudges.
4. **Retain proven repair/accounting semantics.** Weed queue insertion, day reset, stock projection, advanced-sale debt, ten-order cap and terminal realization are preserved where their contracts apply.
5. **Retain dawn logistics.** Automatic cargo deposit and worker reset are part of each programme's exit, avoiding unnecessary generic return-to-shed work while accounting for capacity.
6. **Replace only representation/binding first.** The first reference-like object changes how schedules are specified, not the source service priorities merely for elegance. A later strategy change recompiles the affected dependency closure.

This is not a guarantee of 95%. No author-published schedule generator was found, and all 278 route-0 worker-day sequences are distinct. Recovering a compact, causally useful programme from them is the main work. If the reference-like economy requires recording essentially every original action choice, the representation fails even if its returns match.

### Anti-disguise acceptance rule

The human-authored/searchable object cannot contain a 719-entry joint-action array, a STEP-to-action decision tree, 30 opaque day tapes, or a list of timestamp/worker/opcode edits against Shop. Renaming raw actions as one-instruction skills does not pass.

Require all of the following in the preflight: at least 80% of non-PASS production/service actions arise from semantically named, parameterized visit/lifecycle templates reused at three or more sites/days; no raw exception fragment exceeds eight worker actions; raw exceptions cover <=10% of non-PASS unit actions; and at least four novel economies alter template bindings and dependencies, not only exceptions or SELLs. These are proposed anti-memorization thresholds, not current compression measurements. Movement-path data and scheduling metadata count toward total representation size.

A generated 719-turn expansion is acceptable as **compiler output** for fast evaluation or differential checks only if the authoritative source programme passes that rule and regenerates after meaningful parameter changes without consulting the donor tape. A compiled calendar's existence must not be confused with hard-coding the original schedule. Every emitted action must carry an offline provenance link to its programme/template instance.

## How the Shop basin is escaped

The independent variables are asset programmes, tour membership/order, financing order and branch structure from step 0, not single substitutions on a frozen schedule. There is no required Shop opening, no step-144 lower bound, no fixed lane/site mapping and no one-day retiming limit.

| Degree of freedom | Concrete language change | Coupled reconstruction required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening finance | Replace the initial ordered purchase/sale programme; delay or advance capital commitments | Worker spawns, early crop bridge, feed procurement, later receipt dependencies |
| Labour curve | Change daily hire count, hour and ordering; reassign tours | Spawn geometry and complete worker interval calendar |
| Land | Change expansion phase and site set, including a fourth quadrant | Accessible paths, asset programme starts and financing |
| Crop bridge | Replace finite crop cycles or add tomato/perennial service templates | Seed timing, watering/fertilizer cadence, maturity, replacement and realization |
| Herd mix | Change multiple asset programmes and their placement phases | Feed, care/yield phase, pasture/coop placement, cargo and product-sale schedule |
| Spatial layout | Change contiguous service clusters and tour geometry | Worker roles, path witnesses, input transport and capacity timing |
| Service programme | Alter full visit variants, cadence, input load and harvest batching | Biological constraints and available worker-time intervals |
| Market realization | Replace complete financed sale/deposit sequences | Liquidity dependencies, supply effects, led-sale accounting and terminal stock |

A nominal product such as four opening programmes x four labour curves x four land choices x eight mix choices x eight tour layouts x four realization modes already has 16,384 combinations; richer bindings can exceed a million. This is only an arithmetic description of syntactic capacity. Feasibility and near-elite diversity may be a tiny fraction, which is why the first test measures eight realized economies rather than reporting millions of variants.

Multiple sources contribute **templates and independently justified mechanisms**, not runtime donor IDs. Shop supplies the first service/finance witnesses. Gold/Reactive Router supply alternative recovery semantics, which must be explicitly selected and revalidated rather than mixed invisibly. COK suggests acquisition reconciliation and larger herd/land programmes. Lonespear suggests explicit input-versus-output cargo handling and clustered service. New internal programmes may supply missing tomato, opening and spatial structures. Public source performance is HOLDOUT evidence; fresh self-play establishes BUILD quality.

## State-dependent adaptation

Allow BKG4 decisions at reset, daily boundaries, and named acquisition/transfer completion events. Each branch selects a **complete compatible upcoming programme**, including continuing obligations. Support early branches at 24/48/72 as well as later demand-informed changes; there is no requirement to wait until 144.

Legal branch features include money, actual units, current shed/seeds/cargo, plant/herd state and deprivation, public market inventory/prices, revealed shops, and causally meaningful visible opponent production. Avoid exact public-profile fingerprints and historical identity labels. Integer thresholds and deterministic tie ordering make decisions portable.

Mid-tour interruptions are restricted to declared feasibility failures. Do not freely preempt a feed tour for a speculative purchase. A failure may invoke the declared weed repair, wait within reserved slack, or transfer to a precompiled reduced programme at a checked boundary. Existing livestock/crops retain service ownership. A new mix cannot jump into a different complete schedule after its incompatible assets have already been bought.

At a boundary, settle or carry any advanced-sale debt explicitly. Source Shop already refuses a lead across a 72-step boundary; the new controller must apply the equivalent restriction at any additional strategic boundary, or preserve the exact obligation across it. A branch cannot preview tomorrow's newly observed state to choose today's led sale. Lookahead is permitted only into the currently committed programme; selection using future observations is not.

The first preflight needs live demonstrations of both outcomes of at least two guards, including one strategic choice and one feasibility/recovery choice. Where a natural fresh panel lacks a failure case, use a bounded reachable internal challenge and its actual legal observation. Merely attaching an unexecuted IF node does not establish adaptation.

An exact return-to-Shop fallback is not safe after an incompatible asset change. Existing champion binary routing and protected fallback requirements remain untouched. For experimental new economies, either stay within their explicit obligation-preserving failure graph or mark the run failed. Release integration requires a later explicit contract reconciliation; this research does not weaken protected W-to-L thresholds.

## BKG4 deployment

### Existing capabilities and real limits

The [v4 ABI](../native/bkg4/include/bonsai_kg_v4_abi.hpp) supports conditions, sequences/selectors, reactive variants, `SwitchEnum`, BB assignment/addition, bounded lifecycle effects, waits/timeouts, unit/market intents and movement. It permits 128 BB slots, 512 node-state slots, depth 48, up to 65,534 nodes, 40 units and 128 live intents. A node is 32 bytes; a node-state slot is eight bytes. Those are hard ABI ceilings, not sensible target sizes.

There is **no arbitrary subroutine CALL opcode, general scheduling solver, multiply/modulo expression language, or opaque skill-runtime exemption**. Shared skills need compiler inlining or explicit FSM dispatch. DAY/HOUR features, precomputed constants/tables and bounded state counters replace unavailable arithmetic. `MoveUnitTo` is x-first movement; it is not guaranteed to reproduce source tours or path preferences.

The [VM](../native/bkg4/include/bonsai_kg_v4.hpp) arbitrates unit intents by authority/priority and stable emission order, and sorts market intents by that priority/order. Parallel nodes do not solve joint economic resources. Also, its normal action result uses the actual workforce; Shop's raw tape preserves its requested hand count. Because aggregate PLANT demand can depend on that count, silently normalizing reference actions is not necessarily semantics-preserving.

### Lowering boundary

| Component | Deployed representation |
| --- | --- |
| Observation guards, economic choices, commitment/mode changes | BKG4 conditions, branches, BB effects and explicit FSM dispatch |
| Immutable service/finance programmes and compiled schedule bindings | Versioned programme/route data, included in policy identity and size |
| Programme/tour/branch cursors and state | Actual-sized BB and node-state storage; named ownership |
| Queue insertion, bounded record materialization, ordered stock projection and debt arithmetic | Shared deterministic host/device mechanical helper with declared state |
| Programme generation, constraint solving, source factoring, proposal guidance and economic evaluation | Offline only; no deployed planner/model |

For fidelity, a bounded schedule materializer can emit the source-compatible ordered action packet from programme data, just as today's Shop adapter uses route data after the BKG4 tick. Strategic guards and mode selection remain BKG4-visible. Native helper code may decode selected records and carry mechanical repair/accounting state; it may not rank economies, choose unrequested crops, allocate an undocumented workforce, or replace failed investments strategically.

Register the new programme data format explicitly. The existing BKV6 action record is 94 bytes and supports 12 hands, while the proposed pilot addresses up to 15 hands. Either use a separately versioned variable-length programme/action packet or reject unsupported counts. Do not reinterpret old pack bytes or advertise BKV6 as 40-unit storage. The controller ABI need not change merely because its adapter gains a new bounded programme-data format.

All phenotype evaluation runs the **actual lowered BKG4 controller and materializer**. There is no later approximate projection of the actuator. Future learned guidance may propose programme structures or guard values offline, but it does not alter runtime semantics. This is the main deployment advantage over a learned low-level executor.

## CUDA/snapshot integration

Use the existing [legal observation view](../native/cuda/include/observation.cuh), [v4 adapter](../native/bkg4/cuda/kag_adapter_v4.cuh), shared host/device helper style and exact simulator. Add no policy access to `Config.seed`, RNG tables, opponent private inventories or continuation outcomes. The evaluator can own privileged simulator state; the policy interface cannot expose it.

Upload compiled immutable programme data once per candidate batch. Each world owns its two agents' explicit mutable state. Group similar programme shapes if useful for throughput; candidate ordering and batch size must not change actions. No optimizer or solver runs inside the CUDA kernel. CPU reference and device materializer use identical integer ordering, quantity widths, queue semantics and feature conversion; exact market-price tables remain simulator-owned.

The [CUDA simulator snapshot](../native/cuda/include/sim.cuh) contains `Config` and `State`, rebinding table/RNG-cache pointers on load. It does **not** contain policy state. The generic [snapshot policy runner](../native/cuda/include/policy_runner.cuh) resets policies by default, which is wrong for continuing this stateful representation. Use the ownership pattern from [Phase 1's Fork](../native/cuda/src/phase1_fork_repair.cu), but do not copy its hard-coded two-BB Shop layout.

The new snapshot must include both controllers' sized BB/node states, programme identity and cursor, all queues and queue ordering, led-sale quantities/due steps, branch commitments, pending acquisitions and maintenance handoff state. Store offsets/IDs, not CPU/GPU pointers. Rebind immutable tables by verified hash. Simulator and controller logical state should be serialized canonically; raw C++ padding bytes are not a portable equality test.

Take snapshots at **pre-action** boundaries with an explicit clock convention. Restore must continue without triggering the ordinary new-game/backward-step reset. Compare full logical state and exact ordered actions, not just final cash or the old preflight's partial hashes. Test immediately before/after dawn, route selection, led-sale repayment, a queued weed action and an acquisition handoff. Privileged snapshot fields must never become contract features.

## Immediate representation preflight

**One experiment only: reconstruct and diversify the executable programme language.** No RL, Expert Iteration, neural model, programme-learning campaign or evolutionary search. Manually specify a small frozen witness set through the same source-level language. Static feasibility checks may reject inputs; they may not silently downgrade strategies.

### Objects and design gate

Keep the untouched Shop Plus executable as reference R. Build P0, a source-derived elite-equivalent reconstruction, plus P1-P8. All P objects use the identical programme schema, template semantics, compiler and BKG4/native execution path. They cannot call Shop to fill missing actions. The P0 compiler must operate without access to the reference pack after source factoring.

P0 must reconstruct the observable shop-conditioned behaviour needed for a representative panel, not simply choose the easiest single stored route. Test branch coverage through isolated legal-observation fixtures before simulation. Primitive/template coverage and anti-disguise requirements are checked before treating any cash result as a representation result.

Parameters must be resolved and the objects frozen before the fresh assessment. If eight valid programmes cannot be made inside the cap, that is a failed expressiveness test, not a reason to count different hashes of the same economy.

### Fresh panel and ledger

Use 32 new seed blocks, both seats, with four **live internal self-play/adversarial league configurations** assigned round-robin before results, giving 64 contexts and eight seeds per configuration. Select configurations from approved current internal sources, not new named public agents. They must actually run and react each turn. Record shared ancestry honestly; this is not a generalization/leaderboard-strength panel. Reference R and every P run separately against the same fixed context, so an opponent may react differently to the changed economy.

The seed registry must prove disjointness from existing BUILD/INTERNAL_DEV/downstream panels without opening SEALED payloads. If the registry cannot establish that separation, stop. Label the new assessment panel separately and never use its results to tune a second batch. Research-only reference reconstruction uses authorized source semantics; it does not turn old public outcomes into BUILD labels.

Define **FGE = all joint simulator transitions / 719**, including opponent rollouts, both reference/candidate worlds, CPU mirrors and reconstructed prefixes. A two-agent joint transition counts once, not twice; two separately run games count twice.

Do not regenerate every prefix for free or count suffixes as independent games.

The **eight official-engine games are additional explicit official work**, not hidden in native FGE: P0 and three mechanically most different frozen variants, each in both seats, against a live approved internal counterpart. Compare every action and logical environment transition with native; do not compare only terminal cash. This small canary is a representation smoke test, not the later release canary required by project governance.

### Measuring both axes

Primary execution statistic: `median(P0_cash(context) / R_cash(context))`, with positive reference cash required in every context. Also report ratio of medians, seat/cohort medians and a seed-block uncertainty interval. A nonpositive reference denominator triggers an explicitly reported invalid comparison, not selective deletion or a hidden epsilon.

Diversity uses actual realized state and completed economic programmes at fixed checkpoints, including midday workforce observations. Require each P1-P8 to differ from P0 and the others in at least two economic dimensions, with at least one change in finance/land/labour/production, not only sale ordering or path spelling. Proposed material scales: >=2 assets of a species or >=4 crop tiles, a >=1-day acquisition/land shift, >=2 hands on >=3 days, >=4 genuinely changed service sites, or >=20% timing/quantity change in a financed product programme. The programme must realize its declared commitments, not merely request them.

At least four variants must first differ in an economically operative action before step 144; PASS versus omitted PASS does not count. The witness set as a whole must cover opening finance, labour, land, crop/herd mix, geometry, service and realization. Report cumulative realized production, spend, successful hires, animal/crop losses, failed acquisitions, shed overflow, unsold/cargo stock, tour completion and missed mandatory services.

Count valid distinct economies separately at >=80%, >=90% and >=95% paired retention. As an additional proposed anti-collapse guard, require each of the eight counted variants to retain >=80% paired median cash. This is not the same as proving eight >=95% economies. The primary requested reproduction gate is >=95%; a stronger claim of many near-elite economies requires actually measuring that upper frontier.

The competence/diversity axes belong to the **same frozen batch**. A P0 failure stops the experiment; preliminary P1-P8 probes do not establish success. Conversely, perfect P0 fidelity cannot compensate for eight ineffective or unrealized novel economies.

## Pass criteria

All are conjunctive; no weighted average can compensate for a failed safety or representation condition.

1. P0 passes the anti-disguise rule and achieves **median paired cash ratio >=0.95** on the fresh 64-context panel. Report seat/cohort results; require neither seat median below 0.95 and no cohort median below 0.90 as additional proposed safeguards against pooled masking.
2. **Eight mechanically valid, materially distinct coordinated economies**, at least four with operative divergence before 144, implemented in the **same representation** as P0. Each meets the stated >=80% anti-collapse floor; report how many also reach 90%/95% without claiming unmeasured strength.
3. Realized semantic commitments and all eight specified strategic dimensions are represented across the set; not eight market overlays or copied openings.
4. **Zero CPU/CUDA semantic mismatches, zero snapshot mismatches, zero hidden-information dependencies, zero memory/VM faults**, and no silent worker/order truncation.
5. Two exercised state-dependent guards, one strategic and one feasibility/recovery, with checked resource/maintenance handoff and no unsafe return to a mismatched reference tape.
6. Eight-game official/native canary has zero action/state mismatches. Native work <=1,000 FGE, engineering <=8 days, and measured runtime/storage stay inside the declared caps.

A pass validates a useful policy language, not gold-medal strength, a selected optimizer, release promotion or safety-panel admission. Final protected W-to-L must still be zero through the existing downstream flow.

## Kill criteria

- P0 cannot reach 95% paired productivity inside the fixed time/work budget.
- High productivity needs the entire original 719-step tape, opaque donor days, a STEP-to-action tree, excessive raw exceptions, or hidden fallback calls to Shop.
- Novel programmes cannot be scheduled or repeatedly collapse because service/finance obligations cannot be recomposed; fewer than eight meaningful witnesses pass.
- The compiler has become the failed target/priorities executive, merely moved offline, without preserving complete skilled visits and joint commitments.
- The language is only post-144 local changes, inherited worker lanes or one/two-lifecycle substitutions.
- Contracts require full hidden simulator states, future prices/shops, historical identity fingerprints, or an impractical number of whole-farm-specific cases.
- State-dependent changes abandon obligations, corrupt sale debt/queues, or require unsafe arbitrary mid-tour/full-family switching.
- Essential strategy remains in an opaque native helper or unextractable model rather than declared BKG4/programme data.
- CPU/CUDA exactness, snapshot ownership, workforce bounds or official parity cannot be made exact with the bounded machinery.
- Engineering exceeds eight focused days, deployable data/state exceeds the caps, or evaluator throughput makes the small test impractical.

Do not rescue a representation failure by adding a learner, more public replays, an enormous search campaign, more fallback layers or indefinite compiler complexity. Preserve the failure evidence and stop.

## One fallback

**Candidate B: event-driven BKG4 service-tour skills/FSMs with resource and maintenance ownership.** It is the second-ranked representation, not a concurrent second build.

Trigger only when F's source-factored tour bodies and individual economic programmes are demonstrably competent, deployable and legal, but legitimate timing variation defeats fixed calendar composition: for example weed/acquisition delays repeatedly invalidate otherwise sound boundaries, while observed-state tour completion could preserve the same obligations. Require a bounded counterexample identifying the calendar assumption, not just a low score.

Reuse the factored tours, input contracts and tests; replace absolute calendar dispatch with finite event-driven tour progression and reserved-role ownership. Do not introduce global primitive-task scoring. Allow at most **four additional engineering days and one separately authorized <=1,000-FGE preflight**, with the identical P0/eight-economy/exactness gates. This is only a future fallback specification; no implementation is authorized in this session.

Do **not** trigger it for absent skilled bodies, inability to reconstruct Shop without a tape, insufficient strategic diversity of the language itself, hidden-state dependence, BKG4 infeasibility or general engineering exhaustion. Those invalidate the shared foundation; the correct response is to stop, not automatically spend the fallback budget.

## Path from validated representation to learned discovery

Only after the language passes, choose an optimizer. Candidate proposals should all target the same versioned object: asset programmes, tours, finance/land/labour commitments and legal branch graph. Learned guidance can propose bindings or whole coordinated subprogrammes; population search can recombine dependency-closed programmes; Expert Iteration can label executable alternatives; hierarchical RL can select valid committed programme choices. None requires replacing the actuator or shipping a neural runtime.

Future learning must operate on fresh internal self-play and the adversarial league, with legal observation features and honest opponent responses. BKG4-deployed programmes must be evaluated inside the loop, rather than distilling primitive execution only after an expensive teacher succeeds. Source-aware programme induction can later add a template, but it must bring its concrete executable body, maintenance contract and regression evidence.

Retain the existing safety sequence: **multi-regime BUILD -> discovery oracle -> INTERNAL_DEV -> binary routing -> TRANSFER/VALIDATION/CEGIS/SEALED -> composition**. External policies remain independent HOLDOUT/generalization checks. RLRMB and Direct-Bonsai remain frozen diagnostics. A broad experimental economy is not an automatically safe champion overlay, and its release integration must be explicitly approved without weakening protected zero-W-to-L or exactness requirements.

The next investment is therefore narrow: prove that a compact programme can reproduce detailed elite execution **and** reorganize whole economies. There is no reason to choose or train the strategic learner until that interface exists.
