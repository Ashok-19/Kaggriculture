# Service-tour executable-policy representation preflight

## Verdict

**SERVICE-TOUR REPRESENTATION PREFLIGHT FAIL — EXECUTABLE POLICY LANGUAGE NOT VALIDATED**

The experiment tested the compact resource-contracted service-tour representation and, after a concrete fixed-calendar deadline-binding counterexample, the sole authorized event-driven service-tour FSM fallback.

Stage-1 factoring passed the anti-disguise check: reusable parameterized visit shapes cover 92.87% of productive/service actions; residual raw exceptions are 3.94% of all non-PASS actions and the largest residual fragment is six actions. The P0 source stores no 719-step joint-action tape and no STEP-to-donor-action fallback.

The fixed-calendar compiler failed legitimately under lifecycle timing pressure. The authorized event-driven fallback then recovered substantial competence, reaching 58,777 cash versus 60,011 for Shop Plus (97.94%) on representative seed 61050000. That single-case result did not transfer. On a correctly paired four-seed/both-seat BUILD diagnostic using the same four frozen internal configurations as the earlier representation gate, median P0/reference retention was only 54.24%; seat medians were 54.24%, and cohort medians were 71.98%, 65.36%, 27.87%, and 43.12%. Candidate utility was 0W/0T/8L and median paired cash margin was -50,379.

That is a material P0 failure. Per the explicit kill criterion, the experiment stopped before the 32-fresh-seed panel, P1-P8, BKG4/native lowering, CPU/CUDA parity, snapshots, and official canary. Those gates are **not run**, not silently assumed to fail.

The result falsifies the stronger claim that the current compact site/lifecycle programme plus deterministic event-driven tour binding is already a generally competent executable policy language. It does not falsify Bonsai/BKG4 or native simulator fidelity.
