## Architectural Decision

**Is CCPR recoverable through better trajectory-diverse BUILD evidence? YES.**

The result meets this diagnostic's recovery conditions: multiple known brittle
programs rejected early, one newly refined legalized specialist surviving fresh
DEV with real tie-to-win value, and greater margin/seed support than the frozen
control without safety loss. The root-count increase was zero.

However, this is evidence for the **combined** diversified-seed and conservative
group-guard protocol. It does not isolate seed diversity from stricter ranking,
nor establish that trajectory correlation was the sole cause of Phase-2 failure.
The independent sample per late/early lineage remains only eight discovery and
eight guard games. One profitable conditional body is not a restored claim of
systematic search superiority or an estimate of unseen-regression probability.

Recommend a corrected, still bounded Phase-2 design: unique-seed BUILD groups,
one or two roots per trajectory, group-safe frozen shortlists, an independent
BUILD guard, fresh disjoint DEV, and a properly matched independent-proposal
control including ungated-body controls. Preserve compact WHEN/STOP semantics
and exact parent fallback. Require replicated useful lineages before accepting
the original minimal-CCPR architectural claim. **Do not implement that retry or
DSQD in this session.**
